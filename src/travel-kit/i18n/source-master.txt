<<<HZN0425>>> Start with your own agency
<<<HZN0426>>> This kit supports independent travel advisors and small agencies. Use it for any destination. Choose the language folder that suits the client. The five editions contain the same 40 document pages, not 200 different templates.
<<<HZN0427>>> Unzip the download on a computer. Open Agency_Documents.html in a desktop browser. Enter the agency, client, trip reference, destination, dates and currency at the top. Shared values update across the document pages. Keep the original file as your clean master.
<<<HZN0428>>> Use Worked_Example.html and its eight-page PDF to understand the workflow. Use Operations_Example.xlsx to inspect the calculations, then start real records in Operations_Blank.xlsx. The fictional cities, supplier codes and amounts are teaching data.
<<<HZN0429>>> Edit, save and deliver
<<<HZN0430>>> Click a field to enter text. Choose your agency colour and font in the toolbar. Use the page menu to navigate. Duplicate a daily itinerary or table page when you need more room. A coloured outline warns when text exceeds the page; shorten it or continue on a duplicate page.
<<<HZN0431>>> Save completed copy downloads a new HTML file with your entries, colour, paper size and selection. Reopen that downloaded file to continue. Unsaved edits are not retained when you close the tab. Each language is a separate edition; your client’s answers are not automatically translated.
<<<HZN0432>>> Select only the pages intended for the traveler, then Print selected pages / PDF. The client-page button excludes pages 06, 34 and 38–40, but still review your selection. Choose A4 or Letter, enable background graphics and disable browser headers/footers. Inspect the PDF before sending. Client PDFs are static; calculations remain in the workbook.
<<<HZN0433>>> Run the operations workbook
<<<HZN0434>>> Start in Setup: set one currency and the review date. Changing the currency label does not convert values. The review date controls overdue flags; it does not rebuild historical balances. Use a separate workbook for each currency. Enter real spreadsheet dates, not text.
<<<HZN0435>>> Create a unique trip reference in Trips. Schedule each installment once in Installments. Record each receipt or refund in Receipts using its installment reference; the trip reference is calculated. Pale amber cells are inputs; teal cells contain formulas. Enter 0 where a known amount received is zero. Do not delete formula columns.
<<<HZN0436>>> Use the Overview warning count and each table’s Data check column to fix duplicates, missing inputs and invalid references. Invalid records are excluded from monetary totals. Prepared capacity is 100 trips, 200 installments and 300 receipts. The other limits are 100 inquiries/suppliers and 200 income/expense/task records. Start a new workbook when full; formulas do not automatically extend beyond these limits.
<<<HZN0437>>> Follow the worked example
<<<HZN0438>>> TR-001 has a total of USD 5,600. The three installments are 1,400, 1,960 and 2,240. Receipts of 1,400 and 500 give net client funds of 1,900. The remaining balance is 3,700. On 18 March 2027, only 1,460 is overdue; the final 2,240 is due on 20 April.
<<<HZN0439>>> Expected commissions are calculated separately: 2,350 × 10% × 80% = 188, and 550 × 12% × 80% = 52.80. Total expected commission is 240.80. A separate planning fee of 300 gives expected agency income of 540.80. These expected amounts are not automatically treated as received.
<<<HZN0440>>> Try this in a disposable copy: change the partial receipt from 500 to 1,000. The trip balance becomes 3,200 and the overdue installment becomes 960. Restore the value afterward. A fully paid trip can still need document, insurance, supplier or manual release checks.
<<<HZN0441>>> Refunds, changes and corrections
<<<HZN0442>>> For a refund, add a new receipt ID, the actual refund date, the original installment reference and a negative amount. Preserve the original receipt. A refund of 300 against the sample second installment reduces net receipts to 1,600 and increases the remaining trip balance to 4,000 while the approved price stays unchanged.
<<<HZN0443>>> Cancellation status alone does not calculate a refund or erase the balance. Record the agreed cancellation outcome in page 20. Update the trip total and installment schedule to the documented settlement, then record money actually returned as negative receipts. Check the schedule difference returns to zero. Never infer supplier terms from this template.
<<<HZN0444>>> If an ID is duplicated or an installment is invalid, the affected receipts are excluded until corrected. Resolve the warning rather than overwriting a calculated total. For a genuine duplicate entry, remove only the erroneous input row’s values and preserve its formulas. Keep evidence of corrections in your business records.
<<<HZN0445>>> Client delivery and compatibility
<<<HZN0446>>> Before delivery, complete page 38 and review the exported PDF. Confirm names, dates, currency, price version, payment schedule, supplier references and support contacts. Remove fictional data and internal pages. Send finished client PDFs; keep editable files and income records inside the licensed business.
<<<HZN0447>>> Use a desktop Chromium-based browser for the HTML editor. Save/reopen, text overflow and PDF output were tested in Chromium. The XLSX files use standard formulas. All ten delivered workbooks were independently recalculated in LibreOffice; Microsoft Excel was not directly tested. Google Sheets, mobile editing and cloud collaboration are not included. Keep formulas intact and use your spreadsheet application’s recalculation setting.
<<<HZN0448>>> The 12 email scripts are text you customise and send yourself. Replace every double-brace field before sending. This kit does not book travel, send messages or synchronise supplier systems. For a file problem, contact the seller through your Etsy order and describe the filename and issue without sending sensitive client data.
